Phase 6 Outcomes

  • Build a professional technical portfolio showcasing labs and projects.
  • Create a high-impact, ATS-optimized IT resume.
  • Optimize your LinkedIn profile for tech recruiters and hiring managers.
  • Master the STAR method for behavioral interview questions.
  • Gain confidence in technical interviews and troubleshooting simulations.
  • Learn effective job searching strategies beyond just clicking "Apply".
  • Understand the IT career ladder and how to move from Support to Admin/Security/Cloud.
  • Land your first professional role in IT support!
